# Legend Maker app: build spec

Working name. Started 16 Nov 2026 from the Design Your Pet app. The project
brief is `claude/self-portrait-app-brief.md` in the Goodwookie project.

A customer uploads a photo of one adult, picks a persona, and orders the
artwork. The app shares Design Your Pet's backend, limits, store and checkout;
the website is the source of truth for the generator.

## Decided

- Under the Goodwookie brand, with its own name (Legend Maker for now).
- Self-portraits, gifts and memorials, each with its own recorded promise.
- Built now, released after the 2026 holidays.
- A matching page on goodwookie.com will follow.

## Assumed until Don says otherwise

- The person pictured must be 18 or older.
- One person per photo at launch.
- Tees and hoodies keep the Goodwookie sleeve logo.

## Flow

| # | Screen | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Welcome | "Get started", gallery, designs. |
| 2 | Step 1: Add your photo | Photo; "Who is in this photo?" (me / a gift / in memory); that answer's promise; the AI consent. Continue needs all of them. |
| 3 | Steps 2-3: Choose your legend | One card per persona. Optional name, up to 18 characters, on personas that have a place for it. |
| 4 | Step 4: Generate | About a minute. |
| 5+ | Result, product, cart, checkout, confirmation, designs, orders | Unchanged from Design Your Pet. |

## Whose portrait it is

| Answer | Promise the customer ticks |
|---|---|
| It's me | This is a photo of me, and I am 18 or older. |
| It's a gift | The person in this photo is 18 or older and has agreed to me using it for this. |
| In memory of someone | I am a member of this person's family, or have the family's blessing to use this photo. |

The app sends `who` with every design and only after the promise is ticked.
The website must refuse a design without it and record `who` and the time.
Changing the answer or the photo unticks the promise. The wording above lives
in `WHO_FOR` in `src/data/catalog.ts`; a lawyer should read it before launch.

## Personas

Ten at the start (`STYLES` in `src/data/catalog.ts`): The Monarch, Rock Star,
Movie Star, The Champion, Astronaut, Action Hero, Western Legend, Head Chef,
Explorer, Renaissance Noble. All but the Noble take a name.

Rules for every persona:

- A generic archetype in an invented look. No real team, league, film,
  character, band, brand or celebrity, in the picture or in the name.
- The AI never letters anything. The name is set in type by the website in a
  zone the design leaves empty.
- Clothing is drawn plain: no logos or wordmarks.
- Alive, well and decent: the Design Your Pet content rules all apply.

## Limits

Same as Design Your Pet and enforced by the website: 5 designs and 12 tries
in a rolling 24 hours, 180 an hour site-wide, one design at a time. Expect to
revisit these: faces take more tries than pets.
